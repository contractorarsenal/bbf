## Flooring categories

### Carpet
- Best for bedrooms, media rooms, and other spaces where softness and sound
  dampening matter
- Wide range of piles, fibers, and price points
- Easiest category to stain or wear in high-traffic areas; less ideal for
  homes with pets prone to accidents or for moisture-prone spaces

### Tile
- Extremely durable and water resistant
- Common in bathrooms, kitchens, entryways, and mudrooms
- Cold underfoot without radiant heat; grout lines require maintenance
- Wide design range including natural stone looks

### Vinyl / Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)
- Waterproof or highly water-resistant core options
- Realistic wood- and stone-look visuals
- Durable and low-maintenance — a common recommendation for households with
  pets and kids, or for below-grade spaces like basements
- Generally more affordable than solid hardwood while still giving a premium
  look

### Hardwood (solid and engineered)
- Premium, natural material; adds long-term home value
- Solid hardwood can be sanded/refinished multiple times over its life
- Engineered hardwood has a real wood wear layer over a stable core, making it
  more dimensionally stable in fluctuating humidity than solid hardwood
- Neither solid nor engineered hardwood is recommended for consistently wet
  areas (full bathrooms, basements with moisture concerns) without a
  waterproof underlayment strategy the team should evaluate
- More susceptible to scratching from pets and to water damage than LVP or
  tile

### Laminate
- Photographic wear layer over a composite core
- Budget-friendly alternative to hardwood visuals
- Less water tolerant than LVP; not typically recommended for wet areas

### Waterproof flooring
- Generally refers to LVP/rigid-core vinyl and tile
- Prioritized recommendation for basements, bathrooms, entryways, and homes
  with pets or young kids where moisture or spills are a regular concern

## Installation & removal

- Best Buy Floors offers professional installation as part of a flooring
  project.
- Existing flooring (carpet, tile, hardwood, etc.) can be removed and hauled
  away as part of the project scope when requested.
- Stairs are priced and scoped separately from flat square footage because
  they require significantly more labor per unit.
- Subfloor condition can affect scope and pricing; the team confirms this
  during measurement/site review, not during the initial conversation.

## Financing

- Best Buy Floors offers financing options, including promotional 0% APR

- Specific terms, qualification requirements, and current promotions should

## Kitchen & bathroom remodeling

- Best Buy Floors' showroom scope extends beyond flooring into cabinets and
  countertops, supporting kitchen and bathroom remodeling projects.
- For bathrooms specifically, tile and waterproof resilient flooring are the
  most commonly discussed options given moisture exposure.
